In our lay-led services we follow the traditional liturgy, do not have a [_mechitza_](https://www.myjewishlearning.com/article/mehitzah-separate-seating-in-the-synagogue/), and invite people of any gender to count towards the [_minyan_](https://www.myjewishlearning.com/article/minyan-the-congregational-quorum/). We also welcome members of all Jewish backgrounds and identities and strive to make our traditional practice of Jewish ritual as accessible as possible.<br/><br/>By building anti-Zionist traditional egalitarian Jewish community, we dispute the hold of Zionism on contemporary Jewish identity, provide our solidarity movements with a spiritual home, and move anti-Zionist Judaism from the margins to the center of diasporic Jewish life.
